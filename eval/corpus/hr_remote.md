# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid baseline

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**; office presence is
expected on **Tuesdays and Wednesdays** for team sync days.

## Fully remote contracts

Fully remote positions are granted individually and require a signed remote-work
addendum. Fully remote employees receive a **500 EUR one-time home-office
stipend** and a monthly 30 EUR internet allowance.

## Equipment

Every employee — office, hybrid or fully remote — receives a **company laptop**
as standard equipment. Remote and hybrid employees may additionally request an
external monitor and an ergonomic chair from the IT catalogue.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is limited to **30 calendar days per year** and
requires HR and legal approval at least 4 weeks in advance.
