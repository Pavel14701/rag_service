# Vacation Policy

## Annual entitlement

Every full-time employee receives **25 paid vacation days per calendar year**.
Employees with more than 5 years of tenure receive **2 additional days**
(27 days total). Public holidays are not counted against the entitlement.

## Carrying days over

Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next calendar year;
they expire on 31 March.

## Black-out period

The **entire month of December** is a company-wide black-out period:
vacation requests covering December are rejected unless approved personally
by the VP of Engineering (business-critical on-call coverage).

## Requesting vacation

1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least **10 business days** before
   the start date.
2. Your line manager approves within 3 business days.
3. Vacation longer than 15 consecutive days requires department-head sign-off.
