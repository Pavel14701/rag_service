# Atlas — Frequently Asked Questions

## Warranty

Atlas ships with a **2-year limited warranty** covering parts and labor.
An extended 3-year warranty (total 5 years) is available as a subscription.

## Training

Operator training is a **2-day on-site course**; a self-paced online course is
available free of charge for every customer and covers basic operation,
daily maintenance and error recovery.

## Safety

Atlas is certified according to ISO 3691-4 (driverless industrial trucks).
It stops automatically when a person is detected within a 1.5 m safety zone.
