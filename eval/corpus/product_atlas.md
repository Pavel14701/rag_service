# Northwind Atlas — Mobile Manipulation Robot

## Overview

Atlas is Northwind's flagship mobile manipulation robot for warehouse
logistics. It combines an omnidirectional base with a 6-axis arm.

## Key specifications

- **Max payload:** 12 kg (arm extended: 6 kg)
- **Battery runtime:** up to **8 hours** of continuous operation on a single
  charge; a full recharge takes 90 minutes
- **Top speed:** 1.8 m/s
- **Navigation:** LIDAR + visual SLAM, no infrastructure markers required
- **Operating temperature:** -10 °C to +40 °C

## Fleet management

Up to 40 Atlas units can be coordinated by a single Northwind Fleet Server.
Firmware updates are rolled out fleet-wide in staged waves.
