# Support SLA

## Priority levels and response times

| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Critical** | Production down, no workaround | **1 hour**, 24/7 | 8 hours |
| **P2 — High** | Major feature degraded | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| **P3 — Normal** | Minor issue, workaround exists | 1 business day | 10 business days |

## Coverage

P1 incidents are handled **around the clock (24/7)**, including weekends and
holidays. P2 and P3 tickets are processed during business hours
(9:00–18:00 CET, Mon–Fri).

## Escalation

If a P1 ticket has no first response within 1 hour, it is escalated
e automatically to the duty engineering manager.
