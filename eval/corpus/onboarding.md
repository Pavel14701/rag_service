# Onboarding Guide

## First 30 days

New joiners are paired with an **onboarding buddy** — an experienced colleague
from another team — for the **first 30 days**. The buddy answers everyday
questions and introduces you to the teams you work with.

## Checkpoints

- Day 1: laptop, accounts, MFA enrollment
- Day 7: first small merge request
- Day 30: onboarding review with your manager and buddy

## Access requests

Access to internal systems is requested through the IT portal by your manager;
standard access is provisioned within one business day.
