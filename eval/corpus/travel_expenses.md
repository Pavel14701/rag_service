# Business Travel and Expenses

## Per diem

The daily per diem (per diem) for **Germany** is **60 EUR**; for other EU
countries it is **50 EUR**; outside the EU it is **45 USD**. No receipts are
required for meals covered by the per diem.

## Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to **120 EUR per night** in Germany and
**140 EUR per night** in other EU countries; anything above that requires
pre-approval by your manager.

## Expense reports

Expense reports must be submitted in the finance portal within **14 calendar
days** after the end of the trip, with receipts attached for every item not
covered by the per diem. Late submissions are rejected automatically.

## Flights

Economy class only for flights under 6 hours; business class is allowed for
long-haul flights (6+ hours).
